description: Scan UI files and color pairs for WCAG 2.2 contrast compliance
---

Scan the UI codebase or color pairs for WCAG 2.2 compliance:
1. Normal text must meet >= 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its background.
2. Large text (>= 18pt or bold >= 14pt) must meet >= 3:1 contrast ratio.
3. Eliminate unstyled `:focus` rings and ensure high-contrast `:focus-visible` indicators.
